The actual production of this movie was quite incredible, and though there are some minor details I could nitpick, it was a great cinematic experience overall. 

I think this was a great "action movie." My most common critiques of these movies where one character simply annihiliates hoards of enemies is that it just doesn't feel realistic - why are the terrorists missing thousands of rounds of bullets while this guy's got auto-aim? 

Given the character's story, the plot armor almost comes built in - he's literally destined for this and has divine intervention. The scenes where he's dominantly winning battles or narrowly escaping inescapable defeats are then much more immersive because I don't have to suspend my disbelief the whole time.

I think the Odyssey itself is just a good book with an interesting and classic/iconic plot, so it was fun to see a modern interpretation
