title: "The October Game - Ray Brabury"
rating: 3.1
---

The writing itself is amazing and manages to give a lot of color to what is otherwise quite a lackluster story. My favorite part is the first few pages which give a fantastical and vivid characterization of October, and the way Mich feels about the month. I found the racial element to be interesting but not explored enough to be particularly interesting given the length. The final twist is where it falls flat for me, sort of predictable and not a great conclusion to such an intense build-up.
