This movie was a really beautiful examination of regret. Even though the black and white color scheme threw me off at first, I think it worked well as a medium to convey the movie’s core themes. It’s short and sweet and manages to tell a complete, satisfying, and deeply memorable story in just 1 hour 20 minutes. 

SPOILERS:

The dialogue doesn’t feel particularly captivating or “deep,” but it does such a good job of what it’s trying to. It sounds like high school dialogue pasted onto adults, which is exactly what it is, and that becomes increasingly apparent as the movie goes on. I thought it did a fantastic job of highlighting **why** things could never work while simultaneously showing why it’s so tempting to try.
