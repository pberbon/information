On Wednesday I went to NYC to see Paige. We met up at Grand Central around 10:30 and then went to get Ramen at a nearby store. While there, we talked through some questions from a question randomization web-app that I made for safari. Essentially it just goes through an initial list of 24 questions I added to it, and then we talk about our answers and the person rates the question. There's a live ranking of all the questions and a relegation system for the lowest 3 questions which sends them to the G-league. New questions are added as underperforming ones are removed. I feel like we had generally meaningful discussions and the questions worked well to facilitate that. Afterwards, we went to the Math Museum, which was interesting but felt very unfinished. My favorite exhibit was one where you went back and forth infront of a screen that measured your 2-dimensional position, velocity, and acceleration. There were games where you had to get your position/velocity within certain bands that shifted to the left on the time-x graph which was awesome. Generally, I think the exhibits did a good job building spatial/geometric intuition for mathematical concepts, and the staff were very nice. 

After this, we walked around for a bit and got Matcha. This was Paige's first time trying it, so she got a strawberry one to hedge the risk of it being bad, but unfortunately she was so apathetic to it that she only had ~1/4 of it. Once I had drank ~1/2 of mine, she gave me the rest of hers, so I had a large surplus of Matcha which I mixed together into a mostly strawberry flavored blend. I had some, but since I was initially planning on meeting a friend later, I set aside the rest of the matcha to give her since I had already had enough. That hangout did not end up happening, and so I had extra matcha that was now warm while I was on the train home. I almost always get my drinks cold, and have for a long time, so I thought this would be worse, but when I drank it, I actually found it to be much more flavorful. This was a reminder to never rely too heavily on heuristic thinking and always re-evaluate the mechanisms you use to make decisions. 

On Thursday, I met up with Evan & Dionne for Lunch in NYC. We had Ramen again, as I was inspired by the last great outing to a Ramen restaurant. This place was slightly worse but also slightly cheaper, I think similar value overall. They gave me free sesame oil though which was appreciated. After lunch, I went to see Evan's apartment for the first time and we did some more safari web app questions. Once again it was generally a good time, and I grew to really enjoy the rating aspect as more data was added. We did miscellaneous things for a while, including overviewing the installation of an AC unit, and then Evan cooked up a very scrumptious and incredibly impressive dinner with his chef skills. I was swept off my feet and enjoyed it a lot. We then went to a bar to watch the Sweden-Japan match and then I returned to beautiful Connecticut. 

Friday, Saturday, and Sunday were mostly administrative tasks, touring apartments, and getting things in order. It was more relaxing and felt nice after being out and about a lot, and I got to spend additional time with the Butler family which is always good.
