In the mornings I  typically got a "mixed berry cup" which is blueberries + strawberries and put them in a plain yoghurt with granola, and a vegan banana nut muffin. For some reason I had a tendency to drink the matcha while eating the yoghurt, but I think having it with the banana muffin (one bite + one sip) is much more synergistic because the nutty flavors of the muffin work well with the matcha.
